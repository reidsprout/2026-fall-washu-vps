Writing exercise 01 - What is a ritual, practice, or routine in your life that is important for your psychological wellbeing and/or fulfillment? Why?

I feel like I'm not a very ritualistic person, and I come from a family that doesn't have a lot of traditions or routines. I think we get bored easily. I do have some daily habits when it comes to starting my day. I always check the weather and my email first thing when I wake up... some people are stressed to check their email, and I used to be too, but now I find it exciting, because it's like getting a letter in the mail!

Recently, I've also been waking up early so I have time to get breakfast in Olin Library before my first class. I really like waking up and starting my day in this cafe environment, and seeing all the other early risers. If I don't have last minute homework to catch up on (which is often), I'll usually use the free time to draw or scroll twitter (I wouldn't say the latter is great for my psychological wellbeing, but I'm being honest).

Another practice I have in my life is taking care of my plants. I keep all of them in the sunroom in our apartment and I try to check up on them every couple days, see their growth, and water if needed. It's very calming and slows me down. I feel like they all have different personalities and I'm getting to know them.

Other things that come to mind: rock climbing, cooking with my roommates, calling my mom, birdwatching (during the summer).
