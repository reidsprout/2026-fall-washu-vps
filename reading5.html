Reading 05 - "The Forbidden Zone"

I really connect with what Zucconi says about websites as containers: "for the most part, the reason I make artwork is to put it on the website." While the artwork is not necessarily in itself functional, the creation is motivated by function, and the need to fill a particular space, which I think is similar to how I approach my creative practice.

The discussion of old websites, and how they differ from websites today makes me a little sad. It seems impossible that websites built by individuals will ever dominate the web like they once did. How much have we lost in the name of efficiency?

I'm really, reallyyy excited by the concept behind Zucconi's Dyptichs site, which generates a random pairing between two references he's collected. I could see myself doing something similar in this second project. As an artist, especially when you've collected so many things, you can definitely get stuck sifting through it. Adding an element of randomness to bypass this seems really useful.

I like the talk about container productivity and usefulness - not every container, even if it well designed, will end up getting used. It's important to not only think about form, but also it's broader function and place in your life (or in the intended audience's life, or whoever).
