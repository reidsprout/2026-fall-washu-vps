Media Response 04 - ENO (2024)

I thought this was a really thought-provoking film, both in concept and in execution. I'm always interested in algorithmic art of sorts like this - like using computer programs or other external events to determine output. It seems like Brian is also interested in this. He seems to approach art as a way to respond to existing stimuli, which is very scientific in a way - all hypotheses are formed by observation and a leading question. I think its awesome that human curiousity can lead us down very different paths. A lab environment and a recording studio are so different, but at the same time very similar.

Brian's methodology of setting a system of rules to explore and play within, specifically the Oblique Strategies cards, reminded me of Yoko Ono's performance art and her instructional book Grapefruit. I think the best art is made (or at least starts out as) in reaction to a circumstance, boundary, or constraint. There is simply too much out there that to try create blindly without observing first is pointless.
