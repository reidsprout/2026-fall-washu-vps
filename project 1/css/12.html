syrup, milk, and tea selection

our syrups include vanilla, mocha, caramel, peppermint, hazelnut, lavender, brown sugar cinnamon, churro, white chocolate, and orange & cardamom. you can add syrup to any drink for 75¢.

whole milk is our default, but we offer almond and oat milk alternatives for 60¢.

our teas include earl grey, blushing, chai, mint, green, chamomile, and rose.
