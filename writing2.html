Writing exercise 02 - What is a human-made creation that brings out the best in you? Why?

I think drawing is an obvious answer for me, but I also immediately thought of cooking. Deciding what to cook out of all the miscellaneous things in the fridge really engages my brain and forces me to think actively. When I'm cooking, I feel calmer, present, and really connected to my surroundings and my body. I'm focusing on the food in front of me, not on my phone or anywhere else. It's a relaxing solo activity, but I also really like cooking with others and sharing food. It reminds me to be grateful for the things I have and the people I have to share it with. I'm very lucky to have grown up in a household where both parents cooked and enjoyed cooking, and now I live with two roommates that also love to cook, and are very good at it. Sometimes, it's just about making something warm to eat. But, it can also be a way of expressing yourself or trying something new. It can be a way of reconnecting with your culture or remembering where you're from. It's perhaps because it's so connected to our personal memories and emotions that it seems to exist so far away from technology, and ends up being an escape from that for me.
