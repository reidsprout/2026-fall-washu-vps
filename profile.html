What do you want to learn in this course?

A lot of the internet right now feels like slop, for lack of a better word. I'm interested in learning the technical skills behind UI/UX and combining them with artistic elements to make sites and experiences that feel genuine and handmade, returning to earlier eras of the web.

Tell us about an early experience with computers or the internet

I remember when I was younger I really loved KidPix. My sister and I would sit next to each other at the computer and mess around on it. I also remember my mom showing us tons of music videos on youtube as lunchtime entertainment.

What will the web look like in 10 years from now?

Realistically, I don't have high hopes that the web will not continue to degradate as AI replaces human designers and users. I think the best thing about the internet right now is sites like wikipedia and archives where you can find a lot of reliable, free information, and learn a bunch of stuff! I would hope in the future, as data storage continues to improve, there will be more focus on preserving information and archival efforts.
